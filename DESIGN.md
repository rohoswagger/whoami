## Overview
Preserve the existing personal site's structure and lowercase voice. The approved refresh borrows a few particle.ai colors and adds small original assets. This guidance supersedes the older white-and-blue aesthetic in PRODUCT.md; product facts remain there.

## Colors
Use warm cream for the shared canvas, dark olive ink for primary text, muted olive for supporting text and links, and yellow for selection and home link hover fills. Tailwind's existing gray and blue names now resolve to warm neutrals and olives. Keep the five-step olive data ramp in globals.css for quantitative displays.

## Typography
Figtree carries headings and body text. Geist Mono carries navbar links, data numerals and repository slugs. Retain the bold lowercase home heading, progressively smaller supporting paragraphs, and quiet underlined links.

## Layout
Keep the original single-column home hierarchy: introduction, current work, history, viola, projects, contact. Content sits inside a centered 72rem container with 1rem side padding; the intro caps at 56rem.
The home uses 100dvh minus the main navigation offset: 64px below the desktop breakpoint, 80px from 768px. Compact type and spacing respond to short viewports. Longer routes scroll normally. The fixed navbar retains all route and social links, with tighter mobile spacing.

## Elevation & Depth
The home and navbar share a flat cream background with subtle warm divider lines. Preserve the existing depth treatments on other routes.

## Components
The home brand is a real link with a small original pixel monitor SVG. The viola sentence has a small original pixel viola SVG. Keep these accents near their present 24px scale and preserve their pixel edges; decorative images have empty alt text.
Links retain underline and color feedback. Keyboard focus uses an olive 2px outline with 4px offset. Existing entrance animations honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Do's and Don'ts
Do preserve the established layout, hierarchy, route content and shared palette. Don't copy particle.ai's split-screen composition, window frames or large graphics. Add small original assets only when they support the existing content.
